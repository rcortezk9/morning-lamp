Daily Formation Synthesis.

What is the Church teaching your soul today?

Today the Church calls you to remember that you are not a battleground where light and darkness negotiate — you are a temple, claimed and inhabited by the living God, and He does not share that space. The same voice that silenced the unclean spirit in Capernaum speaks over every unclean thing that has taken up residence in you, and it has no more right to answer back than that spirit did. Do not mistake caution for holiness or willpower for purity; both readings point past your own effort to the authority of Christ, who walks into the exact room where the unclean thing is speaking and ends its claim with a word. Let Him.

Ascetic posture for today: When an unwelcome thought or attachment surfaces, do not argue with it — name it before Christ and let His authority, not your own resolve, be what removes it.
