Daily Formation Synthesis.

What is the Church teaching your soul today?

Today the Church calls you to stop grading yourself on your answers. You have given a great many of them — to God, in prayer, in the promises you make at the end of a Liturgy when everything seems possible — and the Father is not standing at the door listening. He is in the vineyard, watching who arrives. This is not a threat; it is a mercy, because it means the answer you are ashamed of having given is not the record. The son who said "I will not" is the one Christ commends, and he is commended for something so small it barely makes a story: afterward, he went. Whatever you refused this week is still open. Whatever you promised is still waiting. Neither one is settled by anything you said.

Ascetic posture for today: Do one thing you have already agreed to do, without telling anyone you are doing it.
